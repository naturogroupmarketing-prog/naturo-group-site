## Read this before you spend anything

**We are not matching Maid2Match's profile, and you already agreed to that.**
Of their ~500 links, roughly 90% are PBN and link-farm domains. Replicating
that buys a penalty, not a ranking.

**Jim's profile is not reachable by directory submission.** Their links come
from being a national franchise with decades of brand presence. No number of
Geelong listings substitutes for that.

**What is actually achievable** is closing the gap that matters: you currently
have 366 external links, and **274 of them (75%) come from one domain**,
homeimprovement2day.com.au. Roughly five referring domains in total. Ten to
fifteen genuine, varied, local links would be a bigger improvement to that
profile than a hundred directory submissions.

>
> I run NATURO GROUP, an eco-friendly cleaning company working across Geelong
> and the Bellarine, and I am looking at Chamber membership.
>
> Two questions before I pick a tier:
>
> 1. Does a member listing in the online business directory include a live link
>    to our website, or does it link to a profile page hosted on the Chamber
>    site? I want to be clear on what I am getting.

>    not see a cleaning or trades category in the directory.

> Surf Coast.
>
> Thanks,
> Anna Biram
> Managing Director, NATURO GROUP
> 1300 876 472 · admin@naturogroup.com.au · https://naturogroup.com.au

2. Free directories: My Community Directory, Localsearch, Auslistings
3. Check searchgeelong.com.au and greatergeelong.net.au yourself
4. Send 6.2 to ten property managers — the highest-value item here

7. Send 6.4 to two or three clubs

Target is **10–15 genuine referring domains**, not a count. That is what fixes
a profile where 75% of links come from one place.
