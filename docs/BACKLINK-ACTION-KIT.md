## READ THIS FIRST — The Report Is Misleading

The gap report shows Maid2Match with 500 backlinks vs Naturo's 49 — a 10× gap that looks alarming. It isn't. Of the ~100 domains visible in the PDF, **roughly 90% are spam, PBN (private blog network) or link-farm sites** that Google actively penalises. Some examples: `heaven32.com`, `heavenarticle.com`, `everythingbetter.in`, `kashiland.com`, `fngeeks.net`, `usaseodirectory.com`, `powerlinkdirectory.com`, `ahrefs-links.com` (literally fake), `pick4d.live` (gambling), `interactionbetweenprednisonenhw.blogspot.com` (pharma spam), every `.pages.dev` domain, `ranksdirectory.com`, `multiranks.com`, `softranks.com`, `wayranks.com`, `toparticlesdirectory.com`, `machineanswered.com`.

**Do NOT try to replicate those.** If Maid2Match paid an SEO agency for them, that agency built a liability — Google's Penguin/SpamBrain algorithms devalue or penalise this profile at random intervals. If you replicate it you're buying the same liability with your own money.

The real quality gap is much narrower: about **11 legitimate opportunities** from the report, plus ~30 more legitimate AU directories neither of you are on yet.

### Priority Tier 1 (chase this week — highest ROI)

#### 1. bunnings.com.au — Bunnings Tradie Network
- **What it is:** Bunnings maintains a network of local trade & service providers customers can book. Very high domain authority (DR ~85). If Maid2Match is listed, it's likely via the "Find a Tradie" or "PowerPass" service network.
- **Action:** Apply at [bunnings.com.au/trade](https://www.bunnings.com.au/trade) → look for their service provider / trade directory. Sign up for a PowerPass Trade account first (free) — often unlocks access.

## PART 5 — Google Disavow (defensive)

You did NOT show me Naturo's own backlink profile — only Maid2Match's. If Naturo has ~49 legitimate backlinks and no spam, skip this section. But if a prior SEO agency built PBN links to naturogroup.com.au (surprisingly common), you may need to disavow them.

### How to check whether you need this

1. Sign in to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) as the property owner for naturogroup.com.au.
2. Go to **Links** → **Top linking sites**. Download the CSV.
3. Also pull an Ahrefs or Semrush backlink export (free trials work).
4. Review for domains that match the spam patterns you saw in the Maid2Match report (`heavenarticle`, `.pages.dev` blog networks, `authorityprodirectory`, `usaseodirectory`, `ranksdirectory`, `interactionbetween...blogspot`, etc.).

### If you find spam links pointing at YOU, disavow them

## PART 9 — Ranking in AI Search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude, Gemini)

This is the fastest-growing search surface. Cited brands in AI answers earn ~120% more organic clicks per impression than uncited competitors. Called **GEO** (Generative Engine Optimisation) or **AEO** (Answer Engine Optimisation).

### 9.1 What AI search engines index

They pull from:
1. **Traditional web search index** (Bing for ChatGPT + Copilot, Google's index for Gemini + AI Overviews, mixed for Perplexity)
2. **Reddit** — cited disproportionately for local recommendations
3. **Wikipedia** — cited for entity/brand recognition (needs Notability — probably too small yet, revisit at scale)
4. **YouTube transcripts** (Gemini heavily)
5. **Structured data (schema.org)** — how they know "this is a Business, not a blog"

### 9.2 The 6 GEO rules that work

1. **Direct 40-60 word answer at the top of every page.** AI engines quote the first informative paragraph. Example rewrite for the Geelong page opener is in section 8.2 #4 above.
2. **FAQ + Article + LocalBusiness schema** — you already have these in `[suburb].astro`. Ensure they're present on every service/blog page too.
3. **Original stats with citations** — e.g. "According to REIV, 68% of Geelong tenants forfeit part of their bond due to cleaning issues [source]." Engines love numerical claims they can quote.
4. **Third-party mentions** — Reddit, Quora, HouseGrail, Product Review, Trustpilot, industry blogs. Cited brands are the ones that get quoted. Section 8.5 above.
5. **Direct quotations** — include quoted testimonials from real named customers. AI engines lift quotes.
6. **Clean access for AI crawlers** — do NOT block these bots in robots.txt:
   ```
   User-agent: GPTBot
   Allow: /
   User-agent: ClaudeBot
   Allow: /
   User-agent: PerplexityBot
   Allow: /
   User-agent: Google-Extended
   Allow: /
   User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
   Allow: /
   ```
   **Check your current `robots.txt`** at naturogroup.com.au/robots.txt. If any of these are disallowed, that's why you're invisible in the corresponding AI engine.

### 9.3 Per-engine specifics

| Engine | Where it pulls from | What matters most | How to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT (Search)** | Bing index + own crawl (OAI-SearchBot) | Domain reputation, readability score, direct answers | Ask ChatGPT "best eco-friendly cleaner in Geelong Australia" — see if Naturo comes up |
| **Perplexity** | Their own index + Bing | Publisher-friendly (favicon + byline + source card). Weights domain authority + freshness. | Ask Perplexity same query. It shows source cards — check whether Naturo is a source. |
| **Google AI Overviews** | Google's index, prioritises high-authority sites with clear structure | Schema, first-paragraph clarity, E-E-A-T | Google "house cleaning Geelong" — if AI Overview appears, check whether Naturo is cited |
| **Google Gemini** | Google index + YouTube transcripts | Same as AI Overviews + video content | Test as above |
| **Claude Search** | Web search + Anthropic's crawl | Similar to Perplexity — cites publishers | Ask Claude the same query |
| **Bing / Copilot** | Bing index | Bing Places listing, backlinks, freshness | Search on bing.com |

## PART 10 — Ranking on Social Search (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube)

**Why this matters now:** 49% of US consumers use TikTok as a search engine (65% of Gen Z). Instagram + TikTok now outrank Google Maps for local business search among 18-24 year olds. If your Geelong customer base skews under 40 — you need to be findable here.

### 10.1 The 2026 platform shift

| Platform | Search role for a Geelong cleaner |
|---|---|
| **TikTok** | Discovery + demonstration. "cleaning satisfying," before/after content dominates. |
| **Instagram** | Local discovery via geo-tag + hashtag; Reels replicate TikTok surface |
| **YouTube** | Highest-intent AI-search content source (Gemini transcripts). Long-form how-to. |
| **Google Business Profile Posts** | Underused search surface — posts index in Google local pack. Weekly cadence. |

### 10.2 TikTok SEO for a cleaning business

The caption is the SEO lever, not just the hashtags:

- **First 80 characters:** contains focus keyword. E.g. "Deep clean transformation — Newtown Geelong client, 3 hours, non-toxic only."
- **Hashtag mix:** 1 broad (#cleaninggeelong or #cleaningtiktok) + 1 niche (#ndiscleaning, #ecoclean) + 1 geographic (#geelong, #bellarine)
- **Verbal place names in the video** — TikTok transcribes speech. Say "Geelong" out loud once per video.
- **Post cadence:** 3-5x/week for algorithm favour. This is a real time commitment.
- **Content archetypes that work for cleaning:**
  - Before/after transformation (30-60s)
  - "Watch me clean an entire kitchen" (satisfying, long-form)
  - "3 non-toxic products I actually use" (educational)
  - "This is what a nursing-trained cleaner notices" (E-E-A-T + Anna's angle)
  - Customer testimonial reaction shots

### 10.3 Instagram for local

- **Geo-tag every post** with Geelong / specific suburb tag
- **Bio must contain:** "Eco-friendly house cleaning • Geelong & Bellarine" (or wherever you're primarily targeting)
- **Reels > static posts** for search discovery
- **Location-tagged story highlights** for each suburb served
- **Response to every DM within 2 hours** — Instagram weights response rate for ranking

### 10.4 YouTube (for Gemini + long-tail search)

You don't need to be an influencer. You need 10-15 evergreen videos that answer specific search queries:
- "How to deep clean a Geelong beach house after summer" (5-10 min)
- "REIV bond back checklist walkthrough" (10 min)
- "What's actually in eco-friendly cleaning products" (nursing angle, Anna to camera)

Optimise: keyword in title, keyword in first 15 sec of transcript, chapters, description with links to Geelong service page.

### 10.5 Google Business Profile Posts (underused, high ROI)

GBP posts index in Google local pack directly. One post a week takes 5 minutes and beats most paid ads for local visibility.

Post types that work:
